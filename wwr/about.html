At Dry Oar, our mission is to create unforgettable rafting experiences. We believe adventure brings people together and encourages everyone to explore the outdoors.

History

Dry Oar was founded by a group of outdoor enthusiasts who wanted to share their passion for white water rafting. What started as a small operation has grown into a company dedicated to exciting and memorable adventures on the water.
